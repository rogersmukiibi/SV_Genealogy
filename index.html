    About this map

    Every box is a company. Lines show how it descends from earlier ones: founders who left to start it, alumni who joined, money that backed it, or assets it was built from. Almost every line leads back to Shockley Semiconductor Laboratory (1956) and the eight engineers who left it to found Fairchild.

    This first chapter covers 1956–1975. Use the slider or ▶ to watch the Valley grow year by year. Click any company to see its path back to Shockley.

    Sources

    • The semiconductor backbone follows SEMI's 1984 “Silicon Valley Genealogy” poster, developed by Don C. Hoefler from a concept by Jack Yelverton. The poster inspired this map; the map is not a copy of it.
    • Venture firms and non-chip companies are added from the sources listed on each company.
